About me

I grew up in Almaty and wrote my first web page as a first-year student. Since then I have worked on one question: how can technology make public services simpler and fairer?

I led the national open-data platform, taught more than 3,000 students, and now mentor young engineers.
My motto is short: Build it open, build it clear.
